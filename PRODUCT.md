# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Candidates standing at a physical Inblue AI interview kiosk. They are likely under time pressure, using a shared device, and need a low-friction way to enter the 6-character session key provided after booking.

## Product Purpose

Provide a dedicated kiosk entry screen for AI interview sessions. Success means a candidate can immediately understand what to enter, input the session key accurately, and proceed without seeing unrelated mobile app controls.

## Brand Personality

Calm, focused, trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Avoid busy dashboards, marketing-style landing pages, tiny mobile forms, decorative illustrations, and multi-step flows before the PIN entry.

## Design Principles

- One task per screen: entering the session key is the only primary action.
- Kiosk-first clarity: large typography, high contrast, and touch-friendly targets.
- Reduce anxiety: copy should reassure candidates and remind them to use the booking PIN.
- Prevent mistakes: normalize input, show clear character count, and disable submit until ready.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use high contrast, large tap targets, readable text, and clear focus states. Avoid relying on color alone for validation. Keep motion minimal for shared kiosk environments.
